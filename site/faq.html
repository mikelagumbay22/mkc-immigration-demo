How do I get started?

Book a consultation via the contact page or call +1 905-997-3200. You’ll discuss your goals, your current status, and the documents you already have.

What services does MKC help with?

MKC helps with tourist visas and student visas. Clients have also described help with work permits and extensions, permanent residence, and citizenship, alongside general immigration consulting. If your situation is different, ask during your consultation whether MKC can help.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Bring the immigration documents you already have — for example, any permits, letters or prior applications relevant to your situation — plus a list of your questions. MKC will advise what else may be needed.

Can you guarantee an outcome or timeline?

No consultant can guarantee a government decision or processing time. What MKC can do is help you understand your options and prepare your application carefully.

Where is the office?

The office is at 154 Queen St S, Unit 212, Mississauga, ON L5M 2P4, with hours Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Please call ahead to confirm before visiting.

What are the fees?

Fees are confirmed at consultation. No prices are published on this site.
