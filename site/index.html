FAQ

Common questions

How do I get started?

Book a consultation using the section below or call +1 905-997-3200. You’ll discuss your goals, your current status, and the documents you already have.

What services does MKC help with?

Clients have described help with work permits and extensions, permanent residence, and citizenship, alongside general immigration consulting. If your situation is different, ask during your consultation whether MKC can help.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Bring the immigration documents you already have — for example, any permits, letters or prior applications relevant to your situation — plus a list of your questions. MKC will advise what else may be needed.

Can you guarantee an outcome or timeline?

No consultant can guarantee a government decision or processing time. What MKC can do is help you understand your options and prepare your application carefully.
